# sanecmp logo

The mark represents the two independent project components connected through a
small shared interface: sanea in terracotta and sanex in sage.

## Files

- `logo-mark.svg` and `logo-mark.png` — square primary mark for the GitHub organization, repositories and avatars.
- `logo-mark-1024.png` — high-resolution square export.
- `logo-horizontal.svg` and `logo-horizontal.png` — horizontal lockup for light backgrounds.
- `logo-horizontal-on-dark.svg` and `logo-horizontal-on-dark.png` — horizontal lockup for dark backgrounds.

## Colors

- Terracotta: `#BD4A22`
- Sage: `#71886B`
- Ink: `#22272B`
- Ivory: `#FBF8F3`

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the mark width.
Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or separate the connected forms.
